Frequently asked questions

What is Adobe Creative Cloud?

Adobe Creative Cloud provides apps, web services, and resources for all your creative projects — photography, graphic design, video editing, UX design, drawing and painting, social media, and more.

What is the Adobe Creative Cloud Pro plan?

The Creative Cloud Pro plan gives you 20+ creative apps plus Adobe Firefly creative AI, generative credits, fonts, and Adobe Stock assets in one membership.

How do I download and install Creative Cloud?

Sign in with your Adobe ID, download the Creative Cloud desktop app, and install the apps included in your plan.

Are Substance 3D products included with the Creative Cloud Pro plan?

Select Substance 3D apps and assets are available with the Creative Cloud Pro plan; see plan details for what’s included.
